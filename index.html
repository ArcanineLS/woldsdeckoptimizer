Import deck from game
Paste what the game copied (KubeJS script wdo_deck_export.js in kubejs/startup_scripts): Copy for Optimizer on an open deck imports that deck; Copy Collection (next to the JEI search bar) adds every card and deck core you have open (inventory, chest, Card Binder, Tom's Storage terminal, and the cards and cores inside decks) to your collection. The output of /data get entity @s SelectedItem is also accepted for a deck.
